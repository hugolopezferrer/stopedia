Stopedia

Your stops, in the right order.

1. Your HERE key

Stopedia uses the HERE geocoding service to turn addresses into map points. Create a free account at platform.here.com, create a project, and copy your API key here. You only do this once: the key stays saved on this phone, never on the internet.

2. Build a route

Google Maps accepts up to 10 points in total (start + stops + destination), so up to 8 stops per route.

    Open route in Google Maps

    Routes you save stay on this phone. Open one in Maps, edit its stops, or delete it.
