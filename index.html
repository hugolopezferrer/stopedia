Build a route

Google Maps accepts up to 10 points in total (start + stops + destination), so up to 8 stops per route. Filled boxes get a check mark; empty boxes are ignored.

Tip: press and hold the ⠿ handle and drag to change a stop's order — here, and again in the ordered route below.

Add stops from a photo or a list

Scan one address, scan a whole sheet, paste a list, or ask your AI to clean the addresses up. Everything goes through a review screen first: nothing is used until you check it.

Your AI opens with the question already written. Paste your text or attach your photo there, copy its answer (one address per line), and bring it back with “Paste a list”.

Review the addresses

Check each address and fix any mistakes. Rows marked ⚠ look incomplete or badly read. Nothing is used until you tap Apply.

    You need your free TomTom key first

    Stopedia looks up addresses with TomTom, so it needs a key from your own free TomTom account. It takes a couple of minutes, and you only do it once:

    1. Create a free account at developer.tomtom.com (no credit card needed).
    2. Inside TomTom, create an API key and select all the products.
    3. Copy the key itself from the “Key” column of the keys list — not the key's ID.
    4. Paste the key in the field in this tab and tap “Save and test my key”.

    The detailed numbered steps and the key field are right below, in this same tab.

    Your TomTom key

    Stopedia finds addresses with TomTom, a map service. Each person uses their own free TomTom account — address lookups are free up to 20,000 a month and no credit card is needed. Follow these steps once:

    1. Tap the button below to open TomTom and create your free account (your email and a password, on TomTom's own site).
    2. Inside TomTom, go to “API & SDK Keys” and create a key: name it “Stopedia” and select all the products.
    3. In the keys list, tap the key text in the “Key” column: it copies the whole key and you will see “Copied!”. Careful — copy the key itself, not the key's ID.
    4. Come back here, paste the key below, and tap “Save and test my key”. Stopedia checks that it works.
