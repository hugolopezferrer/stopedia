1. Your HERE key

Stopedia finds addresses with HERE, a map service. Each person uses their own free HERE account — about 30,000 free lookups a month. Follow these steps once:

  1. Tap the button below to open HERE and create your free account (your email and a password, on HERE's own site).
  2. Inside HERE, create a project — any name works, for example “Stopedia”.
  3. In your project, find “API keys” and create a key. Copy it.
  4. Come back here, paste the key below, and tap “Save and test my key”. Stopedia checks that it works.
Open HERE and create my free account

2. Build a route

Google Maps accepts up to 10 points in total (start + stops + destination), so up to 8 stops per route. Filled boxes get a check mark; empty boxes are ignored.
